## 6b. Effect of replaying the same payload inside one process

The runner starts one harness process per (AP, config) and replays 5 variants x 5 trials = 25 incidents in it.  P3-L4 / L4b / L5 deliberately keep state across incidents, so later incidents are judged against the earlier replays.  The table splits AgenticCyOps trials by their position in that sequence (position = (variant - 1) * 5 + trial).

| Incident position | Flat ASR | ACL ASR | AgenticCyOps ASR | AgenticCyOps measurable | succeeded | blocked | of which cross-incident-state layers | agent refused |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 (first incident of the run) | 32.4% | 25.1% | **9.1%** | 386 | 35 | 170 | 2 | 181 |
| first trial of each variant (1, 6, 11, 16, 21) | 29.6% | 22.9% | **3.0%** | 1,962 | 58 | 952 | 263 | 952 |
| 2-5 | 34.3% | 26.0% | **0.5%** | 1,544 | 7 | 846 | 253 | 691 |
| 6-10 | 27.5% | 20.3% | **0.7%** | 1,930 | 13 | 957 | 295 | 960 |
| 11-25 | 29.2% | 22.4% | **0.3%** | 5,950 | 16 | 3,120 | 1,232 | 2,814 |
| all | 29.8% | 22.7% | **0.7%** | 9,810 | 71 | 5,093 | 1,782 | 4,646 |

The first-incident row is the only one free of replay state, but it contains variant 1 of each AP only (the most direct phrasing), so it is not a drop-in replacement for the pooled figure either.


Worst-case bound: if every catch by a cross-incident-state layer had instead succeeded, AgenticCyOps ASR would be **18.9%** (1,853 / 9,810).  The true value lies between that and 0.7%; resolving it needs a re-run with per-trial state reset.
